About this plan

    What the sketch model notices (check at the rehearsal)

      Open questions

      How to work on it together

      • Everyone with this link sees the same plan, and every change shows up for everyone within a second or two. Keep the link inside the channel.
      • Turn on Edit. On the map, drag a person to where they should be at the playhead's time: that makes a keyframe (or moves the one already there). Between keyframes people wait, then walk at their pace so they arrive on time.
      • Under the timeline, each person has a row of diamonds (their keyframes). Drag one to change its time; tap it to edit where, facing, what they do and what to watch for.
      • Plan tables holds everything else: marks, beats, the run sheet, cards, swaps, resets, preset, signals, measurements, open questions and the room itself. Edit a cell and it saves when you leave it.
      • Checks lists anything the plan can't read and what the sketch model flags (crowding, walking into counters, who's in frame). History shows who changed what and can undo a change. An hourly copy of the whole plan is kept too: restoring one first saves the plan as it was, so a restore can be put back.
      • Offline on the day? Changes you make are kept and sync when the connection is back.
